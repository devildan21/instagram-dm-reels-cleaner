### 1. Introduction

Enigmatic ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Instagram DM & Reels Cleaner, a Google Chrome extension, accesses, uses, and handles information when you use our extension.

Instagram DM & Reels Cleaner is designed to help users manage and clean their Instagram accounts through tools for bulk deleting direct message conversations, deleting individual messages, unsending messages, and cleaning unwanted Reels.

By installing or using the extension, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

### 2. Information We Access

To provide the extension's functionality, it may access the following information on Instagram:

- **Personal Communications:** Instagram direct messages and conversation content that the extension needs to access to perform user-requested cleanup operations.
- **Website Content:** Instagram page content, including message lists, conversation information, and Reels-related content.
- **User Preferences:** Extension settings and preferences, if users choose to configure them.

The extension accesses only the information necessary to provide its advertised features.

### 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the extension is used exclusively for its intended functionality, including:

- Bulk deleting Instagram direct message conversations.
- Deleting individual messages.
- Unsending messages where supported by Instagram.
- Cleaning and managing Instagram Reels.
- Remembering user preferences and extension settings.

The extension does not use accessed information for unrelated purposes.
